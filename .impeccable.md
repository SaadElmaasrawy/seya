## Design Context

### Users
Content marketers, YouTubers, social media managers, bloggers, and digital professionals — primarily in Egypt and the broader Middle East (bilingual EN/AR). They use SEYA to generate high-quality content rapidly: articles, social posts, scripts, and tweets. They come to the tool with a job to do, so the interface must earn their trust quickly, stay out of their way, and make them feel capable of doing more than they could alone.

### Brand Personality
**Modern. Sleek. Premium.**

SEYA should feel like a high-end tool — not a startup demo. Every pixel signals quality. The brand is confident without being loud, polished without being cold. The voice is direct, intelligent, and professional.

**Emotional goals:** Users should feel Empowered, Focused, and Trustful. The interface should instill confidence that the tool is reliable and capable, reduce cognitive load so users can stay in flow, and create moments where users feel genuinely supercharged by the AI.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Visual tone:** Clean, functional, and precise — inspired by Notion and Linear. Substance over decoration. Dark-first with very deliberate use of color and light.

**What this looks like in practice:**
- Restrained use of color — primaries (blue #3B82F6, purple #8B5CF6) reserved for meaningful actions and highlights, never decorative
- Deep dark backgrounds (#050505–#0f172a) with subtle, purposeful contrast
- Glassmorphism used sparingly — only when layering information spatially (e.g., floating panels, input areas)
- Typography-led hierarchy: Poppins at strong weights (700–900) for headlines, regular weight for body — let scale and weight do the work
- Animations that are functional, not performative — motion should convey state changes or guide attention, never distract
- Generous whitespace; sections breathe

**Anti-references:** Avoid generic "AI startup" aesthetics — loud gradients, rainbow token colors, excessive particle effects, or cluttered dashboards that signal complexity rather than capability.

**Theme:** Dark mode only (default and primary).

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness.** When in doubt, make the action obvious. Labels, hierarchy, and placement should make the next step self-evident without tooltips or tutorials.

2. **Premium through restraint.** Premium does not mean more — it means better. Fewer colors, tighter spacing, crisper type. Resist the urge to add visual elements; earn every pixel's presence.

3. **Motion earns its place.** Every animation must justify itself by communicating state, guiding attention, or confirming an action. Decorative animation is a debt, not an asset.

4. **RTL is a first-class experience.** Arabic layout is not a mirrored afterthought. Spacing, type sizing, and component rhythm should feel equally native in both directions.

5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA compliance is the baseline — color contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 for text, visible focus states on all interactive elements, and meaningful ARIA labeling.
